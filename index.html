01 / A little about me

About
Khun Nanda

I'm Khun Nanda (Sean), an audio engineer and media technology professional based in Chiang Mai, Thailand. With more than 12 years of experience across live sound, broadcast television, and studio recording, I combine technical precision with a calm, collaborative approach to every project.

I'm the founder of 952 LAB, where I provide professional mixing, mastering, and live sound services. Whether I'm shaping a final mix in the studio or running a system on show day, my focus is a clean, reliable sound that serves the artist and the audience.

Alongside 952 LAB, I work part-time as an IT professional with a local organization, keeping systems running smoothly and supporting teams with their daily technical needs.

As a freelance trainer, I also deliver practical workshops for local NGOs and newsrooms. My sessions cover AI for daily use, AI for podcasting, AI for media and video production, and digital security, helping teams build skills they can apply right away.

I believe good work comes from clear communication, dependable systems, and trust within the team. My goal is to help people do their best work.
